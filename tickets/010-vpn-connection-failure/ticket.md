# HD-010 _ VPN Connection Failure

## Ticket Information
Category: Network / Remote Access
Priority: High
Status: Open
User: Landon Wilson
Department: Accounting
Device: Dell Latitude 5420
Operating System: Windows 11

## Issue Description
User reports that they are unable to connect to the company's VPN while working remotely. The user can access the internet normally but receives an error when attempting to establish the VPN connection.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Asked the user when the VPN connection issue began and whether they had connected successfully in the past.
2. Confirmed that the computer had an active internet connection.
3. Confirmed that the user was using the approved company VPN application.
4. Confirmed that the user's company username was entered correctly.
5. Checked the VPN application's connection status and documented the displayed error.
6. Confirmed that the user's account was active and not locked.
7. Closed and reopened the VPN application.
8. Restarted the computer and attempted the VPN connection again.
9. Confirmed that Windows Firewall was enabled and not displaying any related alerts.
10. Checked the computer's date and time settings.
11. Confirmed that the system date and time were synchronized correctly.
12. Attempted to connect to the VPN again.
13. Confirmed that the VPN connection was successfully established.
14. Tested access to an internal company resource after connecting to the VPN.

## Findings
The user's internet connection was working normally, and the company account was active. The VPN application was initially unable to establish a connection. After restarting the computer and reopening the VPN application, the VPN connection was established successfully.

## Resolution
Restarted the computer and reopened the approved company VPN application. The user successfully connected to the VPN and was able to access internal company resources.

## Verification
Confirmed that the VPN connection remained active during testing. The user was able to access an internal company resource successfully and confirmed that remote access was working normally.

## Final Status
**Status:** Resolved

## Technician Notes
The user had a functioning internet connection and an active company account. Restarting the computer and reopeing the VPN application restored the ability to establish the VPN connection. Internal resource access was verified before closing the ticket.
