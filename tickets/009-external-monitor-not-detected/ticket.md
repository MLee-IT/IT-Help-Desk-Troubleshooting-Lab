# HD-009 - External Monitor Not Detected

## Ticket Information
Category: Hardware / Display
Priority: Medium
Status: Open
User: Derick Montana
Department: Sales
Device: Lenovo Thinkpad T14
Operating System: Windows 11

## Issue Description
User reports that their external monitor is not displaying an image when connected to their laptop. The laptop's built-in display continues to work normally, but the external monitor displays a "No Signal" message.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Asked the used when the external monitor stopped working and whether any recent changes were made.
2. Confirmed that the laptop was powered on and operating normally.
3. Confirmed that the external monitor was powered on.
4. Checked the monitor's power cable and confirmed it was securely connected.
5. Checked the video cable connection between the laptop and the monitor.
6. Confirm that the correct input source was selected on the monitor.
7. Disconnected and reconnected the video cable.
8. Tested the monitor's connection using the Windows display settings.
9. Used the Windows display detention feature to search for the external monitor.
10. Restarted the laptop with the monitor connected.
11. Tested the video connection again after the restart.
12. Confirmed that Windows detected the external monitor.
13. Adjusted the display settungs to extend the desktop to the external monitor.
14. Confirmed that the external monitor displayed the laptop's desktop successfully.

## Findings
The external monitor and laptop were powered on, and the video cable was securely connected. Windows initially did not detect the external display. After reconnecting the video cable and restarting the laptop, Windows detected the monitor normally.

## Resolution
Reconnected the video cable and restarted the laptop with the external monitor connected. Windows detected the monitor after the restart, and the display settings were configured to extend the desktop.

## Verificatio
Confirmed that the external monitor displayed the laptop's desktop successfully. The extended display remained active during testing, and the user confirmed that the monitor was working normally.

## Final Status
**Status:** Resolved

## Technician Notes
The issue appeared to be related to the external display connection not being detected by Windows. Reconnecting the video cable and restarting the laptop restored detection of the external monitor. Display functionality was verified before closing the ticket.
