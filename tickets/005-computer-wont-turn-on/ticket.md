# HD-005 - Computer Won't Turn On

## Ticket Information
Category: Hardware / Power
Priority: High
Status: Open
User: Chris Wilson
Department: Operations
Device: Dell Optiplex 7090
Operating System: Windows 11

## Issue Description
User reports that the computer will not power on when the power button is pressed. The monitor does not display an image, and the user does not hear any startup sounds from the computer.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Asked the userr when the computer last worked normally and whether any recent changes were made.
2. Confirmed the power button was being pressed correctly.
3 Checked whether the computerr showed any lights or other signs of receiving power.
4. Confirmed the monitor was powered on and connected properly.
5. Checked the computer's power cable and confirmed it was securely connected.
6. Checked the power cable connection to the wall outlet.
7. Tested the wall outlet with another device to confirm that power was available.
8. Checked the surge protector and confirmed that it was switched on. 
9. Disconnected the computer's power cable and waited several seconds before reconnecting it.
10. Attempted to power on the computer again.
11. Checked for any diagnostic lights or audible beep codes.
12. Confirmed that the computer still showed no signs of power. 
13. Documented the troubleshooting results prepared the issue for hardware support escalation.

## Findings
This computer showed no signs of receiving power after the power cable, wall outlet, and surge protector were checked. No diagnostic lights or audible beep codes were observed. The issue appeared to the computer's power system or internal hardware.

## Resolution
The computer could not be restored to normal operation through basic power and connection troubleshooting. The issue was documented and escalated to the appropriate hardware support team for further diagnosis.
